# External Purchase

Use **+ External Purchase** beside Sales search, or **Buy Externally & Add** on a product card. The modal accepts a manual product or an existing product, including zero-stock products. Quantity can be fractional; purchase cost and selling price are per selected unit. Edit details or quantity in Current Sale. Held carts retain the line snapshot.

External lines use `itemSource: external_purchase`, `inventoryTracked: false`, and a stable `externalPurchaseId`. Checkout validates and computes prices, costs, and totals on the server. It never consumes stock for these lines, even with a product reference. Ordinary products cannot disable stock tracking through a forged request. Existing invoices without the new fields keep their previous behavior.

Each external line creates one sale-linked Expense in the same transaction as checkout. Existing invoice retry protection prevents duplicate posting. Cash, UPI, and Card use their respective ledger channels. Owner / Personal uses OWNER and does not reduce cash or digital balances. Credit / Pay Later stores zero paid amount and the full balance due; it creates no paid ledger movement. Expenses show the pending balance. The current expense architecture does not offer settlement of sale-linked credit expenses separately; an administrator can correct the supplier payment method through the existing commercial-sale edit workflow.

Commercial sale edits recalculate external revenue, cost, profit, and the linked expense without inventory writes. Reordering lines preserves expense identity. Removing an external line from a completed sale retains the actual supplier expense, with a reconciliation note: removing a customer charge does not establish a supplier refund. There is no automatic supplier reimbursement.

Internal sale details show supplier, payment mode, cost, and line profit. Existing sales-create/expense-view permissions authorize cost visibility; read-only sale/customer history responses redact internal supplier/cost fields. Reports expose cost/profit metrics only with existing profitability permission or owner access. Thermal invoices use only item name, quantity, unit, selling price, and selling total. The External Purchase badge and supplier information stay off the receipt.

Sales, overview, and gross-profit reports include inventory product sales and External Purchase revenue/cost/profit. These external metrics use the final billed line totals, including the existing discount/GST pipeline; they do not claim that overall inventory COGS is available. GST-enabled issuance retains the existing requirement for verified product tax classification. A manual item without that classification cannot bypass the fiscal checks; select a correctly classified existing product.

The repository currently has no sale cancellation, customer refund/return, sale deletion, or day-closing endpoint. This feature does not introduce those workflows. The shared stock-return helper excludes external-only sales, and the existing cash ledger deducts cash expenses once. Any future refund/cancellation must refund the customer separately from supplier reimbursement and use persisted inventory allocations for inventory lines.

Validation covers normal and mixed checkout, manual and zero-stock linked items, forged flags/invalid input, multiple costs, retry deduplication, transaction rollback, commercial-sale quantity edits, five supplier payment modes, old sale history, permission redaction, and rendered receipt privacy. Browser interaction, live MongoDB transactions, physical printing, and deployment need separate verification; automated tests use an isolated database harness and never modify live data.
